# GEO — getting cited in AI answers (AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity)

In 2026 a large and growing share of searches show an **AI Overview** above the
blue links, and many people get answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and
Claude without clicking through at all. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is
making your content the thing those AI systems cite. Google's own position is
that optimizing for AI search is still just good SEO — so GEO builds on, rather
than replaces, everything in the other references.

## The foundation: rank, then get cited

AI Overviews draw heavily from pages that already rank well — in practice you
generally need to be in the **top ~10** for a query to be pulled into its AI
Overview. So classic SEO (relevance, authority, technical health) is the price
of entry. GEO is what tips a top-10 page into the citation.

## Different engines retrieve differently

- **Google AI Overviews / AI Mode** → from Google's index.
- **ChatGPT** → leans on Bing's index (so Bing Webmaster matters, not just GSC).
- **Perplexity** → crawls aggressively and cites sources.
- **Gemini** → Google's index.
- Optimize for all of them by being genuinely retrievable, clear, and trustworthy
  rather than gaming any one.

## GEO tactics that work

1. **Answer the question directly, up top.** Lead a section with a clear, concise
   answer (2–4 sentences) and then expand. AI systems lift clean, self-contained
   answers. Use question-shaped headings that match how people actually ask.
2. **Structure for extraction.** Short paragraphs, descriptive H2/H3s, bulleted
   steps and comparisons, tables for data. Make each section stand on its own.
3. **Structured data.** FAQPage, HowTo, and Article JSON-LD help engines
   understand and cite your content. Mark up only what's on the page.
4. **Be a primary source.** Original data, first-hand experience, specific
   numbers, named experts, and citations to primary sources get quoted far more
   than generic rephrasing. This is E-E-A-T doing double duty.
5. **Freshness.** Keep facts, stats and "last updated" dates current; AI answers
   favor up-to-date sources (see `content-and-blog.md`).
6. **Clarity over cleverness.** Define terms, avoid burying the answer, write so a
   machine (and a hurried human) can grasp the point fast.
7. **Earn authority and brand mentions.** The more your brand is cited around the
   web (legitimately), the more AI systems treat you as a trusted source.

## What NOT to do

- No special "AI markup" guarantees a citation — there is no secret tag. Anyone
  selling one is wrong.
- Don't stuff schema or fake FAQs that aren't real questions about the page.
- Don't write for the machine at the expense of the human; thin "AI-bait" content
  fails both.

## Measuring GEO

Track whether your pages appear/are cited in AI Overviews and assistant answers
for your target questions (check manually, or with a reputable GEO monitoring
tool if the user has one). Note that AI-Overview presence is **more volatile**
than classic rankings, so watch trends over time, not single checks. Also watch
that AI Overviews can lower click-through on informational queries — so prioritize
content where being *cited* (brand visibility) and where clicks still happen
(commercial/decision queries) both matter.
